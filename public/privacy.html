Last updated: [ADD DATE]. This is a plain-language starting point. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before you launch.

What PsyConnect stores

  • Your email address and a password (stored as a hash by our login provider, never in plain text).
  • What you choose to record: feelings check-ins, journal entries, saved articles, saved professionals, appointment requests, streaks and unlocked items.

We do not run advertising or sell your data. We do not use analytics trackers.

Who handles it

  • Supabase stores your account and data on our behalf.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

How it is protected

Data is sent over HTTPS and the database is encrypted at rest by our provider. Access rules mean each signed-in person can read only their own entries. Entries are not end-to-end encrypted, so the people who operate the service could technically access stored data. We only do so to keep the service running or when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data from Account, then Download my data.
  • Delete your account and everything saved in it from Account, then Delete my data and account. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Signing out removes your login from this browser. Your session is kept in this browser's local storage while you are signed in.

Not an emergency service

PsyConnect cannot monitor your entries or respond in a crisis. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 or KIRAN on 1800-599-0019 (India, free, 24 hours), or your local emergency number.
